When the user asks for a "summary" or "brief explanation" of a plan, recap, or what's about to happen, keep it to ~5-6 short lines, single section, no markdown headers or sub-bullets. Terse and scannable.

**Why:** User explicitly corrected verbose multi-section summaries twice in one session — asked for "brief summary (short)" after a longer reply. They scan plans separately; the summary is just a sanity check, not a re-render of the plan.

**How to apply:** Use the short form for: pre-approval summaries, end-of-session recaps, "here's what I'll do next" previews. Do NOT shrink: the plan file itself, detailed implementation steps, or end-of-task verification instructions — those need to be complete.
